Privacy Policy

App: Azwad's World (Android, package com.azwadsworld.game)
Developer: Azwad's World
Contact: jarif@parallaxlogic.com
Effective date: 25 November 2026

Summary

  • Azwad's World is a game for young children.
  • We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. We have no servers, accounts, analytics or crash reporting.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob, set up for children's content.
  • Game progress is saved only on the device.

Information saved on the device

The game saves progress (coins, outfits, room items, goals and settings) in the device's local storage. This data never leaves the device and we cannot see it. There is no account, sign-up, chat, social feature, user-generated content or cloud save.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information, and we have no server that receives data from the app. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To protect children:

  • every ad request is marked as child-directed (treated as directed to children under COPPA and under the age of consent for GDPR purposes);
  • ads are limited to the maximum content rating "G";
  • the app does not request the Android Advertising ID permission, so the Advertising ID is not available to the app or the SDK;
  • no personalised (interest-based) advertising or remarketing is used.

To deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, the AdMob SDK may process limited technical information such as the IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, app-instance and app set identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics. This processing is done by Google under its own terms; see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. This information is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we do not receive it in a form that identifies a person.

Some ads are optional "rewarded" ads. The player is always asked first and can say no.

Children's privacy

The game is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, contact us at the address above.

Permissions

The app does not ask for any runtime permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage). It declares internet and network-state access so that ads can load, and wake-lock and foreground-service permissions used by the ad SDK and game engine.

Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on the device and is removed when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared in Android settings. We hold no other data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server. For data processed by Google for ads, see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.
